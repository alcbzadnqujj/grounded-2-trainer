## 📖 About

Grounded 2 by Obsidian Entertainment is the sequel to the backyard survival hit. Shrunk to insect size, you must gather resources, build shelters, and battle creatures many times your size. The sequel expands the world beyond the backyard with new environments and more terrifying insects.
